# LACK guided coach physical pilot protocol

Status: **not_run**. No participants, assembly observations or usability outcomes have
been recorded. This document is a protocol, not study evidence.

Test the exact white LACK 55 × 55 cm article 304.499.08 and tutorial revision
`lack-30449908-coach-v2`. Record source hashes, app commit, browser/device and date for
each session. A change to a physical instruction requires renewed review of affected
actions before testing it.

## Participants and setup

Recruit five consenting first-time assemblers who have not used this tutorial or
assembled this variant. Use the actual matching product and supplied hardware,
protected work surface, official instructions and a phone or laptop. Do not substitute
a different variant. Confirm hardware identity and read safety information before
starting. A facilitator observes and may stop unsafe activity immediately; successful
completion never takes priority over safety. Follow official instructions for care.

Explain that the assistant is a prototype and that progress confirmation does not
certify assembly. Obtain permission for observation and separately for any recording;
use anonymous participant IDs P01–P05. Record only needed observations, with agreed
retention and deletion dates. Do not require accounts or personal information.

## Session procedure

1. Record participant/device and starting familiarity; confirm correct article.
2. Ask the participant to find the product and assemble using the coach. State that
   they can open official diagrams and help or stop whenever uncertain.
3. Observe without coaching. Record action ID, confusion, wrong component/orientation,
   repeated replay, help openings, backtracking, accidental confirmation, focus/layout
   problems and requests for help. Distinguish spontaneous recovery from intervention.
4. Stop and assist on safety-critical misunderstanding; record the issue and exact
   intervention. Do not leave a known unsafe connection for the sake of the study.
5. Ask the participant to perform final fastening checks and describe usage/care
   limits. Record actual observed completion separately from UI completion.
6. Conduct a short debrief: unclear actions, useful views/labels, misleading animation,
   navigation trouble and what would have helped. Record total time descriptively,
   without comparative speed claims.

## Observation record

For each participant record:

- Exact revision, article, source hashes, device/browser, date and facilitator.
- Action ID and timestamp; observation in participant's own words where consented.
- Error or confusion, severity, help used, facilitator intervention and recovery.
- Physical completion observed: yes/no/uncertain; final checks performed: yes/no.
- UI confirmations vs observed actions, including any unperformed confirmed action.
- Safety-critical misunderstandings, resolution and follow-up owner.
- Debrief findings and withdrawal/recording consent where applicable.

Do not silently classify a facilitator-assisted completion as unassisted. Do not
interpret elapsed animation time, stored confirmation or a screenshot as proof of
physical work.

## Proposed gate and revision loop

At least four of five participants finish without facilitator intervention, with no
unresolved safety-critical misunderstanding. Record numerator, denominator and all
interventions. A safety-critical finding blocks the pilot gate until corrected and
retested; do not average it away. Fix other recurring confusion and retest affected
actions with appropriate new participants. Retain original findings and tie each
correction/retest to its revision.

This small study establishes pilot usability only. It does not establish broad
reliability, safe use beyond official limits or comparative assembly-speed claims.
Human source review is a separate signed record identifying the reviewed revision;
physical status may be updated only after actual observed assembly evidence exists.
